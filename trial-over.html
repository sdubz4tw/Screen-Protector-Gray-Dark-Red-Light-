Our Story & Mission

Screen Oasis started because of a simple problem: late-night programming was causing severe eye strain, and existing browser filters were frustrating to use. Standard overlays inverted colors blindly, rendering beautiful dark websites into blinding whites, or blacking out SVGs, icons, and buttons entirely.

Our mission was to build an intelligent visual reader that respects web components. Screen Oasis implements selective class overrides to rescue icons, inputs, and interactive buttons, maintaining site structure while scaling filters to your preference.

This extension is built and maintained independently with care. If Screen Oasis has helped protect your eyes, please consider supporting its development with a donation.
